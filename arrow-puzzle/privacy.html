1. About this policy and the app

Arrow Escape: Tap Puzzle Game is a puzzle game. The app does not require you to create an account, and the game itself does not ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number or precise location to play.

The app is intended for users aged 13 and above. The app may nevertheless be accessed by teenagers under 18. Where applicable, privacy and advertising requirements relating to minors will apply. The age rating or target-audience selection in an app store does not change any rights or protections provided by applicable law.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress and preferences may be stored locally on your device, including level progress, stars, streaks, game settings and daily reminder preferences. This information is used to provide game functionality and is not uploaded to a server by the game according to its current design.

Local information is generally removed when you uninstall the app, although device or operating-system backups may retain data according to your device settings.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information to provide, measure and improve advertising, limit invalid activity and help protect advertising services.

Depending on your device, settings, region, consent choices and the ad features served, information processed by Google may include:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers;
  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate location;
  • Ad impressions, interactions and related app activity;
  • Diagnostic, performance and fraud-prevention information.

The app does not directly receive or control all information that Google processes through its advertising SDK. Google's processing is governed by Google's own terms and privacy information.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tools where required. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be shown a consent or privacy choices message before ads are requested. Where a privacy-options control is made available in the app, you can use it to review or change your choices.

You can also review advertising and privacy controls in your Android device settings. The availability of particular controls may depend on your device, region and Google services.

A consent choice does not prevent all information processing that is necessary for security, service operation, fraud prevention or other permitted purposes.

5. Notifications and reminders

If you enable a daily puzzle reminder, the app may request permission to send notifications and schedule reminders locally on your device. The reminder time and related preferences are used to provide this feature. The app does not use a separate notification account or server to schedule these local reminders.

You can change or disable reminders in the app's settings, or manage notification permission through your device settings.

6. Sharing achievements

If you choose to share an achievement, the app creates or provides the relevant achievement image and opens the sharing options supplied by your device. The image and any information you choose to share are handled by the app or service you select. Their privacy practices apply after you share the content with them.

7. Third-party services and data sharing

The app uses third-party Google services, including Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play services for supported features such as in-app updates and in-app reviews.

Information may be processed by Google in connection with these services, including advertising delivery, consent management, app-update or review functionality, service reliability and fraud prevention. We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a separate database that stores your game progress.

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising information
  • Google Play terms

8. Children's and teenagers' privacy

Arrow Escape is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not designed or marketed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly through the game.

Teenagers under 18 may use the app. Applicable laws may provide additional protections for minors, including requirements concerning consent and advertising. Advertising and data processing are also subject to the settings and safeguards applied by Google and its services. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences stored locally remain on your device until they are cleared by you, removed by uninstalling the app, or otherwise deleted through your device's settings.

Information processed by Google AdMob or Google Play services is retained and deleted according to the relevant provider's policies, settings and legal obligations. We do not control Google's retention periods for information processed by its services.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps within the app's design to limit the information we directly collect. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party services used by the app maintain their own security measures and policies.
