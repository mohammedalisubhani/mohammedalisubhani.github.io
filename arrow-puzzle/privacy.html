Arrow Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Arrow Puzzle is made by Subhani Developers. We respect your privacy. The game does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, settings, streak and reminder time are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to any server.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the game asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system; no notification service, account or server is involved and no data is sent anywhere for this. You can change the time or turn reminders off in Settings, or block them in your device settings at any time.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, IP address and general interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Google Play services (in-app updates and in-app reviews) and Google AdMob. Each has its own privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share an achievement, the badge image is created on your device and handed to the app you choose. Nothing is uploaded by us.

Your choices

You can disable personalised ads in your device settings, manage consent in Settings, or uninstall the game to remove all locally stored data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email subhani.developer@gmail.com.
